Privacy policy

CricPulse · Last updated 28 October 2026

CricPulse lets people score cricket matches, run tournaments and share score cards. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, the name you enter, and a password stored only as a secure hash by our login provider. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Cricket data you add: tournaments, teams, player names and details (role, batting and bowling style, jersey number), logos and player photos you upload, fixtures, and every ball scored.
  • Alerts: in-app notices such as "Fifty card ready", kept so you can open them later.
  • On your phone: matches you score are saved on the device so scoring works without signal.

What is public

Scores, scorecards, points tables, stats, team and player names, logos and player photos are public, so fans can follow matches from a shared link. Your email address is never shown to other people. The name on your account can be seen by others, for example in a tournament's list of organisers and scorers.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • No location tracking.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database, login and file storage, hosted in Singapore). The app itself is served by Vercel. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Players added by someone else

Organisers can add players by name before those players have an account. A player can claim their profile with an invite link to edit it. To have a player profile changed or removed, ask the tournament organiser, or contact us below.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your name on the home screen, then Delete my account. This deletes your login, your roles, your alerts, and any tournament where you are the only organiser, with its matches. Scores you recorded in tournaments run by others stay, without your name attached. Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. Organisers adding younger players should get permission from a parent or guardian first.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/suborhan7/cricket-scoring-app/issues.
